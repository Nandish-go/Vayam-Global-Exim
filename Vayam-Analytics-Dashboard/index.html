READING THE NUMBERS

Signals and outcomes
stay separate.

VisitorAn anonymous estimate. Ad blockers, consent choices and provider methods affect totals.

HandoffA click or prepared enquiry that opens another application. Delivery cannot be proved from the website.

Received leadA real email or WhatsApp conversation recorded privately by Vayam; it is not inferred from site events.

LocationCountry and city estimates can be imprecise. This dashboard contains no raw IP list or named visitor profiles.
