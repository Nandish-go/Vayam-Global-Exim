A USEFUL WEEKLY ROUTINE

Ask a question. Find the evidence.

Are more people finding us?

Compare equal-length periods. Look at Google Search impressions, clicks and the queries that generated them. Then check whether visits from those searches are useful.

Which products deserve more attention?

Choose Product interest in Explore data. Compare the leading groups with the enquiries you actually receive before changing your homepage or catalogue.

Where are visitors getting stuck?

Check website actions and sections reached, then inspect Clarity heatmaps and recordings. Reaching a section means it appeared on screen; it does not prove someone read it.

Are visits turning into enquiries?

Contact handoffs count actions opening email, phone or WhatsApp. They can repeat. Track received messages and qualified buyers privately; website events alone cannot confirm delivery or sales.

How do I interpret a small audience?

Use several complete weeks, keep a note of your testing, and avoid conclusions from one or two visits. Approximate city and country can be affected by networks and VPNs.

Why is a figure unavailable?

A dash means the source did not supply a valid measurement. It is different from a recorded zero. Imported daily data cannot be summed to calculate unique users, and aggregate country tables cannot be re-filtered by date.
