06 · Buyer questions

Frequently asked questions.

Useful answers to the questions buyers most often have about sourcing, availability, samples, packaging, documentation, destinations and quotations.

Can you source products that are not listed on the website?

Yes. The website shows current sourcing directions rather than a fixed inventory. Share the product, specification if known, quantity and destination so feasibility can be assessed.

Are all listed products continuously available?

No. Availability, origin, grade, supplier and commercial terms are confirmed for each enquiry. The website should be read as a sourcing portfolio, not as live stock.

What information should I send for a quotation?

Useful starting points are product name, specification or reference if available, approximate quantity, destination country or port, preferred packaging and target delivery timing.

Do you support private-label or buyer-specific packaging?

Private-label and buyer-specific formats may be possible depending on the product, supplier capability, order quantity, packaging requirement and destination-market labelling rules.

Can you handle mixed-product or consolidated enquiries?

Yes, mixed-product requirements can be evaluated against supplier minimum quantities, packing compatibility, commercial feasibility and shipment planning.

Can samples, testing or product references be arranged?

Where relevant and commercially practical, samples, references, testing or supporting product records can be coordinated as part of the enquiry process.

Which documents are provided for an export shipment?

The document set depends on the commodity, supplier, destination and agreed transaction. Commercial, origin, customs, transport and product-specific documents are aligned accordingly.

Which countries can you supply?

International enquiries are evaluated individually. Destination feasibility depends on the product, applicable import requirements, packing, logistics and commercial conditions.
