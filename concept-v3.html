    Export readiness is not a badge wall.

    It is a transaction-specific system: define what matters, assess the relevant source, align the reference, check the order and coordinate the right records.

    1. 01
      Requirement definition

      Product parameters, destination, application, packing and buyer expectations.

    2. 02
      Supplier + product assessment

      Relevant options, available supplier records and feasible product alignment.

    3. 03
      Reference + verification

      Samples, inspection or testing coordinated where applicable to the order.

    4. 04
      Packing + dispatch checks

      Agreed pack, label, order details and pre-dispatch readiness.

    Document architectureRepresent only what is relevant and confirmed
    A

    Company / exporter registrations

    Applicable business, food and exporter records—kept distinct from product or facility claims.

    B

    Shipment documents

    Commercial, origin, customs and transport records relevant to the consignment.

    C

    Product / destination records

    Testing, inspection, phytosanitary, health or other records where the product and market require them.

    D

    Supplier / facility certifications

    Supplier-level certifications are verified for relevance and never implied as company-wide credentials.
