Privacy & your choices

Development version — 29 September 2026. These four active candidates run without external analytics. The event inspector holds a short, sanitised interaction trace in page memory for testing; it clears on reload.

Your enquiry

Name, company, contact details and requirements are used only to prepare the message you review. They are not sent to a server by this website, stored as a form draft, or included in analytics. WhatsApp and email links pass the draft to the app you choose. You decide whether to send it there. Downloads save the message on your device.

Browser storage

The selected product identifiers are saved in session storage separately for each design and disappear when that browser session ends. The optional analytics choice uses local storage so you are not repeatedly asked. The comparison page can save design feedback locally on this device. It does not upload feedback.

Optional analytics after launch

The prepared Google Analytics adapter is disabled. If it is enabled for an approved production release, the Google tag loads only after you allow analytics. The intended data includes page and product interactions, broad acquisition categories, device and aggregate location information processed by Google, and enquiry handoff actions. Contact fields, free-text searches, messages and full referral/query URLs are excluded from custom events. Analytics cookies are optional. Advertising features are disabled in this implementation.

You can reopen Cookie choices in the footer to decline future analytics. Declining disables collection and attempts to remove the analytics cookies on this domain; it does not delete data already processed by Google. A deployment must confirm the responsible business details, retention settings, applicable notices and service configuration before this draft policy is published.

External services

The embedded Google map connects to Google when its frame loads, independently of the analytics choice. Google may receive technical request data such as your IP address. Google Maps, WhatsApp and your email provider apply their own policies when you use them. Fonts and product images are hosted locally in the four active candidates. The preserved baseline retains its original external image links.
