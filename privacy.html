Privacy and website analytics

Vayam Global Exim is a Bengaluru-based sourcing and export coordination business. You can contact us at info@vayam-global.com.

Measurement status: Google Analytics 4 is configured for this website and loads only after you allow analytics. Microsoft Clarity is prepared but is not enabled in this release. Updated 24 October 2026.

Enquiries

The website prepares your sourcing brief in your own WhatsApp or email application. It does not itself save the form or confirm that you sent the message. If you choose to send it, the recipient application and Vayam will receive the details you included. Please avoid payment-card or sensitive personal information in the brief.

Website measurement

Vayam uses Google Analytics 4 to understand visits, sources, approximate geographic trends, page sections, product-group interest, scroll and visible-time milestones, FAQ opens, form progress and clicks to email, WhatsApp, phone or Maps. Google code loads only after you choose “Allow analytics.” “No thanks” prevents that code from loading. You can change your choice using “Analytics choices” in the footer. Your choice is saved in this browser. Analytics cookies may recognise returning browsers after permission; they do not identify a visitor by name. GA4 derives approximate location from IP information but does not provide Vayam with raw visitor IP addresses.

Microsoft Clarity, if later enabled, can provide click and scroll heatmaps and replays of page interactions after the same permission. Enquiry forms are masked. Clarity is not connected in this release. No Umami tracker is enabled.

Google processes measurement data under its own service terms. Read Google's business data privacy information and Microsoft's privacy statement. Browser privacy signals such as Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable this site's optional measurement. Withdrawing permission stops future measurement; it does not erase earlier reports automatically. Contact Vayam for questions or applicable data requests.

The site does not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, message text, quantities, precise destinations, raw search terms, full URL queries or raw referrers as custom event details. A section view or enquiry handoff is a sign of interest, not proof that the visitor read every word or sent the message.

Other services

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. The office map is provided by Google and may contact Google when it loads; use the separate directions link if the map does not load. WhatsApp, email and Google Maps links open their respective services when selected. Those services apply their own terms and privacy practices.

Your request

For a question about information you sent directly to Vayam, or to ask for correction or deletion where applicable, email info@vayam-global.com. Vayam will review the request against the relevant business and legal obligations.
