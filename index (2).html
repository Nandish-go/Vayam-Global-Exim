About Vayam Global Exim

Vayam Global Exim is a Bengaluru-based B2B sourcing and export business focused on Indian food products. We work from buyer requirements rather than presenting every product as permanently available.

Product form, quantity, destination, packing and documentation needs are aligned with feasible sourcing options before a commercial offer is confirmed.

Sourcing base: Bengaluru, with access to wider Karnataka and Indian supply networks.
Buyer focus: Importers, distributors, wholesalers, food-service and buyer-led programmes.
Working approach: Clear requirement capture, supplier coordination, documentation and dispatch readiness.

Buyer questions

Practical answers for common buyer questions.

Do you support mixed grocery shipments?

Yes, the intended model includes consolidated shipments with multiple Indian grocery categories, depending on minimum order quantities and export feasibility.

Can products be packed under private label?

Private-label support may be possible depending on supplier capability, order volume, packaging requirements and destination-country labelling rules.

Which countries do you export to?

We evaluate international enquiries by destination based on product suitability, applicable import requirements, packing, logistics and commercial feasibility. A destination is confirmed per enquiry.

What information is needed for a quotation?

Please share product list, quantity, destination port/country, preferred packaging, target price range and any certificate or labelling requirement.
