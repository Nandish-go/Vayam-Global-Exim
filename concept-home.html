02 · Sourcing & quality

Quality begins before the order.

Vayam’s role is to translate a buyer requirement into a structured sourcing brief, coordinate the right supplier conversations, and keep product, packing and documentation aligned through dispatch.

01

Define

Product, grade, application, volume, packing, destination and target parameters are clarified.

02

Source

Potential suppliers and product options are assessed against the brief, not a generic catalogue.

03

Verify

Samples, available supplier records, inspection or laboratory testing are coordinated where applicable.

04

Align

Packing checks, commercial details and pre-dispatch documents are matched to the confirmed order.

Buyer-visible decision points

Before quotationRequirement match

Product form, target parameters, volume, packing and destination are converted into one working brief.

Before confirmationReference alignment

Samples, feasible packing, supplier records and any buyer-requested checks are clarified where applicable.

Before dispatchOrder readiness

Agreed inspection or testing, pack and label checks, and the relevant document set are coordinated.

05 · Compliance & documentation

The right document for the product, supplier and destination.

Export readiness is not a badge wall. Requirements vary by commodity and market, so documentation is identified and coordinated for the actual transaction.

A

Company & exporter records

Applicable business, food and exporter registrations, confirmed before being represented.

B

Shipment documentation

Commercial, origin, customs and transport documents relevant to the consignment.

C

Product & destination records

Testing, inspection, phytosanitary, health or other records where the product and market require them.

D

Supplier-level certifications

Facility or process certifications are treated as supplier-specific and verified for relevance.
